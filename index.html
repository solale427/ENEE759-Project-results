Project Results

Based on the GIFs and plots, the proposed method identifies aggressive driving behavior more clearly than KDSC and TDBM. The detected aggressive trajectories show sharper turns, stronger lateral motion, and more abrupt maneuvering, which better match the visual behavior observed in the scenarios. In comparison, KDSC and TDBM often miss these aggressive patterns or assign them less consistently.
